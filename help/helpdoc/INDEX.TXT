Type  HELP <topic>   e.g.  HELP STACK      (pages live in /HELP)

Every entry on these pages carries [x] or [ ]. [x] means the word IS in
this machine and [ ] means it is not yet - and as of 2026-08-04 that is
not a hand-kept promise: each page was probed word by word against the
live dictionary, so the two lists agree with what FIND-NAME can see.
417 of 588 are present. The unticked remainder is mostly whole modules
(FLOAT, GRAPHIC, AUDIO*, LOADSAVE) plus the four files durexforth.asm
does not assemble - INPUT, KEYBOARD's last three, CONTROL's clock and
I2C - each of which says so on its own page.

BANK and KERNAL are gone from this index, and from the /HELP directory.
They documented the 6502 machine underneath the X16: an 8 KB window at
$A000 with a bank register behind it, and calls into a KERNAL sitting in
banked ROM. The 65816 addresses all sixteen megabytes flat and there is
no ROM here past the boot page, so those pages described a machine you
could not reach from this one. Where the data went instead: the
dictionary spans program banks $01-$04 (HELP MEMORY) and bulk data lives
in SDRAM through FAR-ALLOC and friends (HELP MEMORY, FAR-*).
