this machine and [ ] means it is not yet - and as of 2026-08-04 that is
not a hand-kept promise: each page was probed word by word against the
live dictionary, so the two lists agree with what FIND-NAME can see.

(FLOAT, GRAPHIC, AUDIO*, LOADSAVE) plus the four files durexforth.asm
